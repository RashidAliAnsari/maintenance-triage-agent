# Property Management Agreement — Maintenance Authority

Northgate Property Management LLC
Agreement between Property Manager and Property Owner
Schedule C — Repair Authority and Spending Limits

---

## Section 1 — Delegated Authority

**1.1** The Owner authorises the Property Manager to arrange and pay for repairs
and maintenance to the Property without prior consultation, subject to the
limits set out in Section 2.

**1.2** The Property Manager shall exercise reasonable commercial judgement in
selecting contractors and shall obtain value for money on the Owner's behalf.

**1.3** All expenditure under this authority shall be recorded and reported to
the Owner in the monthly management statement.

---

## Section 2 — Spending Limits

**2.1 Standard authority.** The Property Manager may authorise any single repair
up to and including **five hundred dollars ($500)** without reference to the
Owner.

**2.2 Owner approval required.** Any single repair with an estimated cost
**exceeding five hundred dollars ($500)** requires the Owner's written approval
before work is instructed. The Property Manager shall provide the Owner with a
description of the defect, at least one quotation, and a recommendation.

**2.3 Emergency exception.** Where a defect presents an immediate risk to the
safety of occupants, to the structure of the Property, or of substantial further
damage, the Property Manager may instruct works exceeding the standard authority
without prior approval. The Owner shall be notified within twenty-four hours and
provided with a full account of the works instructed and the reason for
proceeding.

**2.4 Aggregation.** Related works arising from a single defect shall be treated
as one repair for the purposes of this Section and may not be divided to remain
within the standard authority.

**2.5 Annual cap.** Total maintenance expenditure under standard authority shall
not exceed two percent of annual gross rental income without the Owner's
approval of a revised budget.

---

## Section 3 — Contractor Selection

**3.1** The Property Manager shall engage only contractors holding current
public liability insurance of not less than one million dollars and, where the
trade requires it, current licensing.

**3.2** For works estimated above five hundred dollars, the Property Manager
shall obtain a minimum of two quotations where time permits.

**3.3** The Property Manager shall not engage a contractor in which the Property
Manager holds a financial interest without disclosing that interest to the
Owner in writing.

---

## Section 4 — Response Standards

**4.1** The Property Manager shall respond to reported defects according to the
urgency classification set out in the Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure.

**4.2** Where the Property Manager is unable to secure a contractor within the
applicable response window, the matter shall be escalated to the Owner with an
explanation and a proposed alternative.

---

## Section 5 — Recovery from Tenant

**5.1** Where a repair is determined to be the Tenant's responsibility under the
lease, the Property Manager shall arrange the works and recover the cost from
the Tenant, either by direct invoice or by deduction from the security deposit
at the end of the tenancy.

**5.2** The Property Manager shall not withhold a repair necessary for
habitability pending recovery of cost from the Tenant.

**5.3** Where responsibility is disputed or unclear, the Property Manager shall
refer the matter to the Owner before allocating cost to the Tenant.

---

## Section 6 — Matters Reserved to the Owner

The following shall in all cases require the Owner's prior written approval,
regardless of cost:

- Replacement rather than repair of a major system, including the heating
  system, the roof covering, or the electrical consumer unit
- Any structural alteration
- Any works requiring building control approval or planning permission
- Any settlement of a Tenant claim for compensation
- Engagement of a contractor on a retainer or ongoing service agreement
