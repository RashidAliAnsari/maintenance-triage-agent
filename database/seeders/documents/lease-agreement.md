# Residential Lease Agreement — Maintenance and Repairs

Northgate Property Management LLC
Standard Residential Tenancy — Maintenance Provisions
Revision 4.2, effective January 2026

---

## Clause 12 — Landlord Maintenance Obligations

The Landlord shall maintain the premises in a habitable condition and shall be
responsible for the repair and replacement of the following at the Landlord's
expense:

**12.1 Structural elements.** The roof, exterior walls, foundation, floors,
ceilings, load-bearing walls, staircases, balconies, and window frames. Any
crack, subsidence, water ingress, or structural movement is the Landlord's
responsibility regardless of when it is reported.

**12.2 Plumbing systems.** All supply pipes, waste pipes, drains, water heaters,
sanitary fixtures, and their fittings, where failure arises from age, wear,
material defect, or system failure. This includes leaking pipes, failed water
heaters, and loss of hot or cold water supply.

**12.3 Electrical systems.** All fixed wiring, consumer units, circuit breakers,
sockets, switches, and permanently installed light fittings. Any electrical
fault presenting a safety risk — including exposed conductors, burning odours,
scorch marks, or repeated breaker trips — is the Landlord's responsibility and
must be reported immediately.

**12.4 Heating and cooling.** The central heating system, air conditioning
units, thermostats, ductwork, and associated controls, where installed by the
Landlord. Seasonal servicing is arranged by the Landlord annually.

**12.5 Appliances supplied with the unit.** Any appliance present in the unit at
the commencement of the tenancy and listed on the inventory schedule, where
failure arises from normal use or mechanical fault. See Clause 15 regarding
appliances still covered by manufacturer warranty.

**12.6 Common areas.** Corridors, stairwells, shared entrances, external
lighting, refuse areas, and any grounds not demised exclusively to the Tenant.

---

## Clause 13 — Tenant Maintenance Obligations

The Tenant shall be responsible, at the Tenant's expense, for:

**13.1 Damage caused by misuse or negligence.** Any damage arising from the
Tenant's act, omission, or negligence, or that of the Tenant's household,
visitors, or pets. This includes but is not limited to broken windows, damaged
doors, holes in walls, stained or torn floor coverings, and damage from
overloaded electrical circuits.

**13.2 Blockages caused by improper use.** Drain and toilet blockages arising
from the introduction of items unsuitable for disposal, including wipes,
sanitary products, cooking fats, food waste, or foreign objects. Where a
blockage is found on inspection to arise from root ingress, pipe collapse, or
accumulated scale, responsibility reverts to the Landlord under Clause 12.2.

**13.3 Consumable items.** Replacement of light bulbs, fuses in portable
appliances, smoke alarm batteries, extractor filters, and water filter
cartridges. Vacuum filters, refrigerator water filters, and HVAC filters are
consumables under this clause.

**13.4 Internal cleanliness and ventilation.** Reasonable ventilation of the
premises to prevent condensation. Mould arising solely from inadequate
ventilation and the Tenant's failure to heat the premises reasonably is the
Tenant's responsibility. Mould arising from a defect in the building fabric,
penetrating damp, rising damp, or a plumbing leak is the Landlord's
responsibility under Clause 12.1.

**13.5 Tenant's own property.** Any appliance, fixture, or item installed or
brought into the premises by the Tenant, and not listed on the inventory
schedule.

**13.6 Loss of keys.** Replacement of lost keys and any resulting lock change.

---

## Clause 14 — Reporting and Access

**14.1** The Tenant shall report any defect promptly upon becoming aware of it.
Where a delay in reporting causes the damage to worsen materially, the Tenant
may be held responsible for the difference in repair cost.

**14.2** The Tenant shall permit access for inspection and repair upon
twenty-four hours' notice, save in an emergency where immediate access may be
required without notice.

**14.3** The Tenant shall not arrange repairs directly without the Landlord's
prior written consent, except where an emergency presents an immediate risk to
persons or property and the Landlord cannot be reached.

---

## Clause 15 — Appliances Under Manufacturer Warranty

**15.1** Where an appliance supplied with the unit remains within its
manufacturer warranty period, a defect shall be addressed through a warranty
claim rather than by engaging a contractor. Neither party bears the repair cost
in such cases.

**15.2** The Landlord maintains a schedule of appliance purchase dates and
warranty terms. Refer to the Appliance Warranty Schedule for current coverage.

**15.3** Where a warranty claim is refused on the grounds of Tenant misuse,
responsibility falls to the Tenant under Clause 13.1.

**15.4** Where the warranty period has expired, responsibility falls to the
Landlord under Clause 12.5.

---

## Clause 16 — Disputed Responsibility

**16.1** Where responsibility for a repair cannot be determined from the
circumstances reported, the Landlord shall arrange inspection by a suitably
qualified contractor before assigning cost.

**16.2** Pending determination, the Landlord shall carry out any repair
necessary to maintain habitability or prevent further damage, without prejudice
to the final allocation of cost.

**16.3** Where the Tenant disputes an allocation of responsibility, the matter
shall be referred to the Property Manager for review within fourteen days.
