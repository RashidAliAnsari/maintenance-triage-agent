# Contractor Rate Sheet

Northgate Property Management LLC
Agreed Rates — Approved Contractor Panel
Effective January 2026, reviewed annually

---

## Standard Hourly Rates by Trade

| Trade | Standard rate | Out of hours | Emergency callout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plumbing | $85 per hour | $128 per hour | $150 plus hourly |
| Electrical | $95 per hour | $143 per hour | $165 plus hourly |
| HVAC | $90 per hour | $135 per hour | $160 plus hourly |
| Appliance repair | $75 per hour | $113 per hour | Not offered |
| General contracting | $65 per hour | $98 per hour | $120 plus hourly |

Standard rates apply Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. Out of hours rates apply
evenings, weekends, and public holidays. Emergency callout is charged in
addition to the hourly rate and applies where attendance is required within four
hours.

---

## Minimum Charges

All trades charge a minimum of one hour. Time is thereafter charged in
increments of thirty minutes.

A no-access charge equal to one hour at the standard rate applies where the
contractor attends at an agreed time and cannot gain entry.

---

## Typical Job Durations

These estimates support cost forecasting. Actual duration varies with the
specific defect.

**Plumbing**

- Dripping tap or replacing washers: 1 hour
- Clearing a blocked sink or basin waste: 1 to 2 hours
- Clearing a blocked toilet or soil stack: 2 hours
- Repairing a leaking supply pipe: 2 to 3 hours
- Replacing a water heater element or thermostat: 2 to 3 hours
- Full water heater replacement: 4 to 6 hours

**Electrical**

- Replacing a faulty socket or switch: 1 hour
- Investigating a tripping circuit: 1 to 2 hours
- Replacing a light fitting: 1 hour
- Rewiring a single circuit: 4 to 6 hours
- Consumer unit replacement: 6 to 8 hours

**HVAC**

- Thermostat replacement: 1 to 2 hours
- Servicing or recharging an air conditioning unit: 2 to 3 hours
- Clearing a blocked condensate drain: 1 to 2 hours
- Replacing a heating circulation pump: 3 to 4 hours
- Full system diagnosis where no heating: 2 hours

**Appliance repair**

- Diagnostic visit: 1 hour
- Replacing a dishwasher pump: 2 hours
- Replacing an oven element: 1 to 2 hours
- Replacing washing machine bearings: 3 to 4 hours

**General contracting**

- Patching and making good plaster: 2 to 3 hours
- Easing and adjusting a door: 1 hour
- Replacing a lock cylinder: 1 hour
- Investigating a roof leak: 2 to 4 hours
- Damp and mould treatment, single room: 4 to 6 hours

---

## Materials

Materials are charged at cost plus fifteen percent. Contractors shall provide
receipts for any single material item exceeding fifty dollars.

Where materials for a job are expected to exceed two hundred dollars, the
contractor shall notify the Property Manager before purchase.

---

## Trades Not Covered by the Panel

The following require engagement outside the standard panel and are quoted job
by job rather than hourly:

- Roofing works involving scaffold or access equipment
- Gas appliance servicing and certification
- Pest control
- Asbestos survey or removal
- Structural engineering assessment

Requests falling into these categories should be escalated rather than assigned
automatically.
